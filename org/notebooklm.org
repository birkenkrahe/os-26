* In-class Exercise: From Source to Project Plan (NotebookLM)

** Goal

Use a *single external source* and NotebookLM to identify *concrete first
steps* for a possible course project, then represent those steps
visually.

** Time-boxed workflow (50 min)

*** 1. Find one source (15 min)

Individually:

- Find *one substantial online source* relevant to the course
  (article, technical blog post, paper, documentation page).
- The source must be *technical*, not promotional.

Write down:
- Title
- URL
- One sentence explaining why this source could support a project

Upload the source into NotebookLM.

*** 2. Analyze with NotebookLM (15 min)

In NotebookLM, ask:

#+begin_quote
Based on this source, what would be the *first 3–5 concrete steps*
to turn this into a small student project?
#+end_quote

Then ask:

#+begin_quote
What assumptions or prerequisites does this source imply?
#+end_quote

Do *not* ask for a proposal or a full project plan.

*** 3. Produce a visual artifact (15 min)

Create *one* of the following (your choice):

- a *single infographic*
- *one slide* (not a deck)
- a *simple mind map*

The artifact must show:
- a project title
- the 3–5 first steps
- at least one assumption or risk

You may use any tool (Google Slides, PowerPoint, draw.io, paper +
photo).

*** 4. Submit (5 min)

Submit:
- the visual artifact
- the source link

Be prepared to explain your artifact in *30 seconds*.

** What we will discuss (brief)

- Are the “first steps” actually actionable?
- Which sources led to clearer project structure?
- Where did NotebookLM help, and where did it overreach?

** Notes on the artifact

- A *single slide or infographic* is usually the most effective format.
- Mind maps are acceptable, but often sprawl and hide assumptions.

** Summary

- Assumptions: source quality matters; NotebookLM output requires filtering.
- Risks: vague sources; steps that are really goals; visuals without decisions.
- Human judgment required: source selection, step concreteness, feasibility.
